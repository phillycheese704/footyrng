THE PLAYBOOK

Controls & platform tips.

🖥️ PC keyboard and mouse

Click any button, or use Tab / Shift + Tab and Enter. Arrow keys navigate buttons. R rolls or rerolls, C customises a completed card, T opens your team, Esc goes back, and ? opens these controls.

🎮 Controller (when supported by the browser)

D-pad / left stick move focus, A / ✕ select, B / ◯ back, X / □ roll or primary action, Y / △ squad, LB / RB switch tabs, LT / RT change club pages and Menu / Options open controls. Use left/right to change a focused dropdown, and A to move to its next option.

📱 iPad and touch

Tap to interact, scroll to see all sections, and use landscape or portrait. An iPad-compatible Bluetooth controller may work if Safari exposes it to the Gamepad API. You can also add the GitHub Pages site to your Home Screen.

💾 Saves and hosting

Progress saves locally in this browser. Use Backup and Restore to move it between devices. Every 8th roll is Golden (4.75× luck); every 100th roll is a Cosmic Roll (15× base luck), replacing Golden if both land together. Existing saves start their Cosmic counter at zero. Xbox Edge may work with controllers; other consoles may limit web access.
